KNOWLEDGE IS POWER

Understanding Domestic Violence

Domestic violence can take different forms. Recognizing harmful behaviour can help someone understand their situation and seek appropriate support.
🛑

Physical Violence

Physical actions that cause or threaten physical harm.

💬

Emotional Abuse

Repeated behaviour intended to control, humiliate or intimidate another person.

💰

Financial Abuse

Controlling access to money, employment or financial resources.

📱

Digital Abuse

Misuse of phones, social media or technology to monitor, threaten or control someone.
